## Event Overview

Global Game Jam 2026 Shenyang site concluded successfully in February 2026. As part of the world's largest game creation marathon, the Shenyang site attracted over 150 developers from across Northeast Asia.

## Highlights

### 48-Hour Creative Sprint

Participants created game prototypes from scratch in just 48 hours. This year's theme was "**Echo**", perfectly aligning with Permafrost Echo's brand philosophy.

### Featured Works

The Game Jam produced 22 game prototypes across various genres:

| Genre | Count | Notable Works |
|-------|-------|---------------|
| Platformer | 6 | Echo Jump, Mirror World |
| Puzzle | 5 | Sound Maze, Frequency Resonance |
| Narrative | 4 | The Last Echo, Memory Fragments |
| Action | 4 | Sonic Chronicles, Resonance Strike |
| Others | 3 | Echo Farm, Rhythm Garden |

## Looking Forward

Permafrost Echo will continue hosting Game Jam events, with the next edition planned for Fall 2026. We look forward to more developers joining us in growing the Northeast Asia indie game ecosystem!
