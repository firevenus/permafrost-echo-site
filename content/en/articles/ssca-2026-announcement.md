## Event Announcement

Permafrost Echo is proud to announce that **SSCA 2026** (Shenyang Indie Game Creators Alliance Annual Showcase) will be held on **March 15, 2026** in Shenyang.

## Event Highlights

### Scaled Up

Compared to the first SSCA 2025, this year's event has been fully upgraded:

- **Exhibited Titles**: Increased from 8 to 12+
- **Participants**: Expected to exceed 500
- **Coverage**: Expanded from Shenyang to the entire Northeast Asia region
- **Partners**: 5 new brand sponsors added

### Event Content

1. **Game Showcase** — 12+ indie games available for hands-on demo
2. **Developer Talks** — 5 keynote speeches and roundtable discussions
3. **Business Matchmaking** — One-on-one meetings between publishers and developers
4. **University Alliance Forum** — Exchange and collaboration among representatives from 12 universities
5. **Award Ceremony** — Annual Best Indie Game Selection

### Exhibited Titles (Partial)

- 🎮 *Doomsday Traveler* — Post-apocalyptic survival adventure
- 🎮 *Phantom Blade Chronicles* — Cyber-Chinese action RPG
- 🎮 *Star Garden* — Healing idle simulation
- 🎮 *Void Chess* — Hardcore strategy chess
- 🎮 *Gravity Shift* — Innovative puzzle platformer
- 🎮 *Frost Punk Tales* — Northeast industrial aesthetic narrative adventure
