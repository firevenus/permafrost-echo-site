## Event Announcement

Permafrost Echo is proud to announce that it will host the **Game Zone** at the **17th Shenyang SSCA Animation, Comic & Game Expo** in **October 2025**, marking our debut in organizing the game exhibition area.

The SSCA Animation, Comic & Game Expo is one of the largest anime, comic, and game conventions in Northeast China, attracting tens of thousands of ACG enthusiasts and gamers each session. Permafrost Echo's debut as the game zone operator provides indie game developers from Northeast China with a showcase platform directly facing the general gaming public.

## About SSCA Animation, Comic & Game Expo

SSCA (Shenyang Animation, Comic & Game Expo) is the most influential ACG convention in Northeast China, held multiple times a year. It features cosplay performances, anime merchandise, game demos, doujin creation, and more. The venue is typically the Shenyang New World Expo (K11) or the Liaoning Industrial Exhibition Hall.

As the game zone operator at SSCA, Permafrost Echo is dedicated to carving out a dedicated showcase space for indie game developers within large-scale commercial conventions, enabling more players to discover and experience indie game creation from Northeast China.
