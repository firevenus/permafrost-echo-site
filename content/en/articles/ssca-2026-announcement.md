## Event Announcement

Permafrost Echo is proud to announce that it will host the **Indie Game Zone** at the **SSCA Shenyang Animation, Comic & Game Expo** on **March 15, 2026** in Shenyang.

The SSCA Animation, Comic & Game Expo is one of the largest anime, comic, and game conventions in Northeast China, attracting tens of thousands of ACG enthusiasts and gamers each session. Permafrost Echo has been invited to curate an indie game zone within the expo, providing indie game developers from Northeast China with a showcase platform directly facing the general gaming public.

## Event Highlights

### Scaled Up

Compared to our debut appearance, this year's game zone has been fully upgraded:

- **Exhibited Titles**: Increased from 8 to 12+
- **Participants**: The expo attracts over 10,000 visitors; the game zone expects to host 500+ demo players
- **Coverage**: Expanded from local Shenyang developers to the entire Northeast China region
- **Partners**: 5 new brand sponsors added

### Event Content

1. **Game Showcase** — 12+ indie games available for hands-on demo
2. **Developer Talks** — 5 keynote speeches and roundtable discussions
3. **Business Matchmaking** — One-on-one meetings between publishers and developers
4. **University Alliance Forum** — Exchange and collaboration among representatives from 12 universities

## About SSCA Animation, Comic & Game Expo

SSCA (Shenyang Animation, Comic & Game Expo) is the most influential ACG convention in Northeast China, held multiple times a year. It features cosplay performances, anime merchandise, game demos, doujin creation, and more. The venue is typically the Shenyang New World Expo (K11) or the Liaoning Industrial Exhibition Hall.

As the game zone operator at SSCA, Permafrost Echo is dedicated to carving out a dedicated showcase space for indie game developers within large-scale commercial conventions, enabling more players to discover and experience indie game creation from Northeast China.
